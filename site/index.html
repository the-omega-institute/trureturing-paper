01

Setting

Conjugation acts on a golden integer in coordinates by sending (a, b) to (a + b, −b). The certified carrier theorem records the invariant that survives this action.

02

Certified result

Conjugating a golden integer preserves its integral trace. In coordinates, conjugation sends `(a,b)` to `(a+b,-b)`, and both traces simplify to `2a+b`. The declaration is certified by the frozen release.[1]
