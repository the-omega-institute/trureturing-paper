# Novelty gate

The gate rejects self-scoring and prose-only declarations of novelty. At least
two nearest prior results must have been checked within the preceding year.
Each row records the prior statement, exact change in hypotheses, exact change
in conclusion, method delta, a counterexample or boundary analysis, mathematical
significance, source-check date, and support status.

A viable main contribution must survive four tests:

1. The theorem differs materially from its nearest comparators.
2. The delta survives stronger-statement and counterexample searches.
3. The central statement has verified Lean evidence with assumptions and axiom
   closure available for audit.
4. The consequence is falsifiable. A reader can identify what becomes provable,
   computable, classifiable, or reusable because of the result.

A formalization report alone does not satisfy the gate unless the formalization
resolves a recognized mathematical obstacle or contributes independently useful
infrastructure. Failure returns to Intuition and theorem development. Rewording
the contribution or selecting a weaker venue does not change the result.
